# Verlune — Visual V4.1 Material Pass

Status: `IMPLEMENTED_CANDIDATE / BROWSER_ACCEPTANCE_OPEN`

Date: 2026-09-24

## Intent

Do not redesign the website.

Move the central visual system from:

```text
clean SaaS hero with a 3D object
```

toward:

```text
premium spatial product scene made from dark glass, light and information
```

while preserving the established Verlune layout, copy, routes, entitlement and product scope.

## Home hero

The central object remains three primary slabs, but the material contract changes:

- substantially thinner extrusion;
- larger depth gaps between layers;
- rounded/beveled perimeter;
- smoked dark surfaces;
- deliberate transmission;
- physical thickness and IOR;
- clearcoat with low roughness;
- restrained attenuation tint;
- stronger top-surface rim than lower layers;
- controlled internal mint light;
- subtle underside illumination;
- top-surface reflection treatment;
- lower slabs fade into depth.

The WebGL runtime stays demand-rendered and retains the existing accessibility/reduced-motion fallback.

## Lighting

The previous high-intensity green point source was reduced.

V4.1 uses:
- neutral studio key;
- soft cool fill;
- restrained mint internal light;
- limited highlight source;
- lower global tone-mapping exposure.

The objective is optical depth rather than visible green glow.

## Semantic cards

Cards remain informational DOM layers.

Their material now uses:
- darker smoked background;
- lower border contrast;
- stronger top highlight;
- deeper neutral shadow;
- less emissive mint;
- reduced connector brightness.

## Premium visual continuity

Premium remains DOM/CSS rather than WebGL.

A shared smoked-glass token layer now applies to:
- Premium semantic graph;
- graph slabs;
- graph information blocks;
- Builder cards;
- Workflow cards;
- Prompt rows;
- Toolkit cards;
- public Premium capability cards;
- Premium summary panel;
- mini semantic objects.

This is visual unification, not a structural redesign.

## Performance boundary

No new continuous animation loop was introduced.

The Home WebGL scene keeps `frameloop="demand"`.
Premium uses CSS/DOM materials only.
