# Content Strategy Brief

Status: `PREMIUM PROMPT CANDIDATE / NOT_FOR_SALE`

Use to turn an audience, offer, and communication goal into a focused content strategy rather than a list of random post ideas.

## Prompt

```text
Build a content strategy brief from the information below.

BRAND / CREATOR / PROJECT
{{identity}}

AUDIENCE
{{audience}}

OFFER OR DESIRED OUTCOME
{{offer_or_goal}}

CURRENT CONTENT / CHANNELS
{{current_state_or_none}}

KNOWN AUDIENCE PROBLEMS / QUESTIONS
{{evidence_or_unknown}}

CONSTRAINTS
{{time_budget_channels_voice_compliance_or_none}}

RULES
- Do not invent audience pain, market evidence, customer results, testimonials, or platform performance.
- Treat unverified audience assumptions as hypotheses.
- Strategy must connect content to an observable audience or business objective.
- Avoid publishing volume as a strategy by itself.
- Separate discovery, trust, conversion, and retention jobs where relevant.
- Reuse is allowed; repetitive ideas/angles are not.

PROCESS
1. Define the audience and desired behavior.
2. Separate evidence from audience hypotheses.
3. Define 3–5 content jobs/pillars.
4. Define what each pillar helps the audience do.
5. Map formats/channels to those jobs.
6. Define a sustainable cadence.
7. Create an initial experiment backlog.
8. Define signals that would validate or invalidate the strategy.

OUTPUT
1. Strategy thesis.
2. Audience evidence / hypotheses.
3. Content pillars.
4. Channel/format roles.
5. Editorial rules.
6. 2-week test plan.
7. Metrics tied to the objective.
8. Risks and invalidation signals.
9. Next research needed.

VERIFICATION
Before finalizing:
- every pillar must connect to a stated audience problem, question, or explicit hypothesis;
- every metric must connect to the stated objective rather than vanity alone;
- observed audience evidence and hypotheses must remain distinguishable;
- repeated topics/angles must not be presented as new strategy;
- no customer result, market fact, or performance claim may be invented.
```
