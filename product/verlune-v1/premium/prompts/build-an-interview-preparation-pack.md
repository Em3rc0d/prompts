# Build an Interview Preparation Pack

Status: `PREMIUM PROMPT CANDIDATE / NOT_FOR_SALE`

Use when a real job description and real candidate history should become a focused interview-preparation package.

## Prompt

```text
JOB DESCRIPTION
{{job_description}}

RESUME / EXPERIENCE
{{resume_or_experience}}

KNOWN INTERVIEW FORMAT
{{format_or_unknown}}

TIME AVAILABLE
{{time}}

AREAS OF CONCERN
{{concerns_or_none}}

RULES
- Use only experience, skills, projects, and results actually supplied.
- Do not invent achievements, metrics, technologies, responsibilities, or employers.
- Map preparation to explicit role requirements before generic interview questions.
- Identify gaps honestly and prepare ways to discuss them without fabrication.
- Prefer evidence-rich stories over memorized slogans.
- Separate likely questions from known interview topics.

PROCESS
1. Extract role requirements.
2. Map candidate evidence to each requirement.
3. Identify strongest and weakest evidence areas.
4. Build evidence-based story prompts.
5. Generate technical/behavioral preparation questions appropriate to the role.
6. Prepare gap-handling responses.
7. Create a final review plan.

OUTPUT
1. Role/evidence matrix.
2. Priority preparation areas.
3. Evidence stories to rehearse.
4. Likely question set, labeled as inference.
5. Gap-handling guidance.
6. Questions for the interviewer.
7. Final preparation checklist.

VERIFICATION
Every candidate claim in the preparation pack must trace to supplied resume/experience evidence.
```
