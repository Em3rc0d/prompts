# Master a Topic

Status: `PREMIUM WORKFLOW CANDIDATE / NOT_FOR_SALE`

Workflow ID: `VP-WF-006`

Use when the goal is durable understanding and transfer, not merely receiving an explanation.

## Inputs

Required:

- topic;
- desired level/outcome.

Useful:

- source material;
- deadline;
- current background;
- assessment format;
- time per session.

## Mastery states

Track each concept as:

- `NOT_SEEN`
- `FRAGILE`
- `RECALLABLE`
- `APPLICABLE`
- `TRANSFERABLE`

Do not promote a concept based only on the learner saying it feels clear.

## Workflow

### 1. Define mastery target

Clarify what the learner should be able to explain, solve, compare, design, or apply.

### 2. Dependency map

Identify prerequisites and the smallest useful concept sequence.

### 3. Diagnostic

Use short retrieval/application tasks to estimate current state.

### 4. Teach

For one concept at a time:

- mental model;
- precise definition;
- example;
- contrast/non-example;
- link to prerequisites.

### 5. Retrieve

Require the learner to reconstruct the idea without copying.

### 6. Apply

Use a new example or problem.

### 7. Challenge misconception

When an error appears, identify the incorrect model and design the smallest contrast that repairs it.

### 8. Transfer

Ask the learner to apply the concept in a different context.

### 9. Update state

Promote or demote the mastery state according to observed performance.

### 10. Schedule next action

Choose the next concept, revisit, or delayed retrieval check.

## Output per session

1. Current target.
2. Mastery map.
3. Teaching unit.
4. Retrieval/application challenge.
5. Feedback after learner response.
6. Error/misconception ledger.
7. Next action.

## Fallback

If prerequisite knowledge, source material, or scope is insufficient:

- keep the relevant concept at `NOT_SEEN` or `FRAGILE`;
- identify the smallest missing prerequisite or source;
- do not manufacture a mastery result;
- propose the next bounded learning action.

If the requested topic is too broad for one coherent mastery cycle, split it into prerequisite units before proceeding.

## Boundaries

- Do not invent facts from missing study material.
- Do not falsely mark mastery.
- For graded assignments, teach the reasoning rather than impersonating the learner.
- For current factual topics, distinguish learning workflow from source research.

## Verification

A topic is `TRANSFERABLE` only after the learner applies it correctly in a meaningfully different case.
