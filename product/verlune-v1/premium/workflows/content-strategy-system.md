# Content Strategy System

Status: `PREMIUM WORKFLOW CANDIDATE / NOT_FOR_SALE`

Workflow ID: `VP-WF-007`

Use when content must operate as a repeatable system tied to an audience and objective rather than as isolated post generation.

## Inputs

Required:

- creator/brand/project;
- target audience;
- primary objective.

Useful:

- offer/product;
- channels;
- current content;
- audience evidence;
- constraints;
- available production capacity;
- prior results.

## Evidence boundary

Separate:

- `OBSERVED` audience/analytics/customer evidence;
- `SOURCE_CLAIM`;
- `HYPOTHESIS`;
- `UNKNOWN`.

Do not invent customer pain, performance, testimonials, platform data, or product outcomes.

## Workflow

### 1. Define audience progress

What should the audience understand, do, trust, or choose differently after engaging with the content?

### 2. Evidence audit

List what is actually known about the audience and what is still hypothesis.

### 3. Define content jobs

Create a bounded set such as:

- discover;
- understand;
- trust;
- evaluate;
- act;
- retain/refer.

Only keep jobs relevant to the objective.

### 4. Build pillars

Each pillar must:

- solve a recurring audience question/problem;
- support one or more content jobs;
- generate multiple non-duplicate angles.

### 5. Channel role

Define why each channel exists. Do not prescribe a channel simply because it is popular.

### 6. Editorial system

Define:

- formats;
- cadence compatible with resources;
- quality bar;
- evidence requirements;
- repetition controls;
- CTA rules.

### 7. Experiment backlog

For each experiment:

- hypothesis;
- content;
- audience signal;
- metric;
- continue/stop condition.

### 8. Produce planning window

Create a small, executable batch rather than an infinite calendar.

### 9. Review loop

After real results are supplied:

- compare observed signals to hypothesis;
- preserve winning patterns without duplicating topics/angles;
- retire weak assumptions;
- update strategy.

## Output

1. Strategy state.
2. Objective and audience.
3. Evidence vs hypotheses.
4. Content jobs.
5. Pillars and angle rules.
6. Channel roles.
7. Editorial operating rules.
8. Initial experiment backlog.
9. Short planning window.
10. Measurement and review loop.
11. Known risks / unknowns.

## Strategy states

- `READY_TO_TEST`
- `READY_WITH_HYPOTHESES`
- `NEEDS_AUDIENCE_EVIDENCE`
- `NEEDS_OFFER_CLARITY`

## Verification

Every recommended content direction must connect to an audience need/hypothesis and an observable objective.
