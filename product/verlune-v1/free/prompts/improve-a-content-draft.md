# Improve a Content Draft

Status: `FREE CANDIDATE / NOT_FOR_SALE`

Use when a social post, article section, script, newsletter, or other content draft needs a stronger reader experience.

## Prompt

```text
Improve this content draft without manufacturing authority or hype.

DRAFT
{{draft}}

FORMAT / CHANNEL
{{linkedin_blog_video_script_email_other}}

AUDIENCE
{{audience}}

GOAL
{{educate_explain_persuade_start_conversation_other}}

VOICE
{{voice_or_keep_current}}

FACTS / CLAIMS THAT MUST NOT CHANGE
{{constraints_or_none}}

RULES
- Preserve factual meaning.
- Remove generic filler, repetition, and empty transitions.
- Strengthen the opening only when it truthfully represents the content.
- Prefer concrete language and useful specificity.
- Do not invent numbers, testimonials, customer pain, outcomes, credentials, or urgency.
- Keep the result appropriate to the stated channel and audience.

OUTPUT
1. Revised draft.
2. Strongest improvement made.
3. Weakest remaining section, if any.
4. Claims that require verification before publishing.
```
