# Prepare for an Interview

Status: `FREE CANDIDATE / NOT_FOR_SALE`

Use to prepare for a job interview using the actual role and your real experience.

## Prompt

```text
Help me prepare for this interview using only experience I actually have.

ROLE / JOB DESCRIPTION
{{job_description}}

MY BACKGROUND
{{resume_experience_projects_or_summary}}

INTERVIEW TYPE
{{recruiter_behavioral_technical_case_unknown}}

AREAS I AM WORRIED ABOUT
{{optional}}

RULES
- Do not invent experience, achievements, tools, employment, metrics, or credentials.
- Identify likely question themes from the role description.
- Help me connect my real evidence to those themes.
- If I lack evidence for an important requirement, help me answer honestly rather than fabricate.
- Avoid protected-characteristic assumptions about employers or candidates.

OUTPUT
1. What the role appears to prioritize.
2. My strongest relevant evidence.
3. Gaps I should prepare to discuss.
4. 8 likely interview questions.
5. Suggested answer structure for each, using my real evidence.
6. Three questions I can ask the interviewer.
7. A short mock-interview sequence.

When I answer a mock question, critique clarity, relevance, evidence, and concision.
```
