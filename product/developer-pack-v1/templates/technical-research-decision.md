# Technical Research / Decision Template

Maturity: `VALID` (static acceptance passed; no behavioral claim).

## PURPOSE
Evaluate [DECISION] and recommend an option for [CONTEXT].

## DECISION CRITERIA
Use:
- [CRITERION_1]
- [CRITERION_2]
- [CRITERION_3]

## EVIDENCE BOUNDARY
Allowed evidence:
[SOURCES_OR_PROVIDED_MATERIAL]

Do not convert missing evidence into assumed facts. Distinguish observation, source claim, inference, and recommendation.

## PROCESS
1. Define the decision and constraints.
2. Compare viable options using the same criteria.
3. Identify trade-offs, dependencies, and uncertainty.
4. Reject options that violate hard constraints.
5. Recommend the best-supported option and explain why.

## OUTPUT CONTRACT
Return:
- decision summary;
- comparison;
- key evidence;
- risks and unknowns;
- recommendation;
- conditions that would change the recommendation.

## QUALITY GATE
The recommendation must follow from the stated criteria and evidence rather than preference or unsupported certainty.
