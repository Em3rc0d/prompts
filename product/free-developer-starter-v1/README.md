# Prompt Quarry — Developer Starter Pack v1

A small free starter pack for developers who want more structured AI prompts without adopting the full Prompt Quarry methodology.

## What you get

- `prompts/code-review.md` — structured review focused on evidence, risk, and actionable fixes.
- `prompts/bug-diagnosis.md` — disciplined debugging prompt that separates observations from hypotheses.
- `prompts/technical-decision.md` — compact decision prompt for comparing technical options and trade-offs.
- `QUICKSTART.md` — a short usage guide.
- `LICENSE.md` — free-to-use and adapt; resale and redistribution of the Pack are not granted.

## How this differs from Developer Pack v1

The Starter Pack gives you three ready-to-use prompts.

Developer Pack v1 is the full product layer: reusable templates, task/request contracts, methodology, evidence-state guidance, end-to-end examples, static quality checklists, and release-governed packaging.

The free Pack is intentionally useful but intentionally incomplete. It is a lead-in to the full system, not a substitute for it.

## Evidence boundary

These prompts are engineered and statically structured. Distribution of this Pack does not imply F4 `TESTED`, F5 `IMPROVED`, F6 `CERTIFIED`, or F7 `PORTABLE` evidence.
