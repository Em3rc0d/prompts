# Human Editor Skill

**Artifact type:** skill  
**Status:** derived-analysis  
**Primary inspiration:** Alpacka AI Threads collection `DURUbidDrA5`  
**Official source:** https://www.threads.com/@alpacka.ai/post/DURUbidDrA5

## Purpose

Turn a mechanically written or AI-assisted draft into prose that reads naturally while preserving meaning, factual claims, intent and audience fit.

This is a **derived reusable skill**, not a verbatim copy of the source prompts.

## Inputs

- `draft`: text to review
- `audience`: who will read it
- `intent`: what the text must accomplish
- `voice`: desired voice or existing author voice
- `constraints`: facts, terminology, formatting or claims that must not change

## Procedure

1. **Preserve truth first.** Identify statements, numbers, names and commitments that must remain unchanged.
2. **Detect mechanical patterns.** Flag repetitive sentence shapes, generic transitions, symmetrical paragraphs, unnecessary restatement and over-explanation.
3. **Repair rhythm.** Vary sentence length and paragraph density where it improves readability.
4. **Restore voice.** Prefer concrete language and phrasing appropriate to the specified audience and author voice.
5. **Remove artificial polish.** Delete filler, inflated claims and formulaic conclusions that do not add meaning.
6. **Reader pass.** Read the draft as the target reader and repair confusing transitions or unnatural phrasing.
7. **Final integrity check.** Confirm no facts, requirements or intended meaning were lost during rewriting.

## Output contract

Return:

1. `revised_text`
2. `material_changes` — short list of the most important edits
3. `preserved_constraints` — facts/requirements intentionally kept intact
4. `remaining_risks` — anything that still needs human verification

## Guardrails

- Do not invent personal anecdotes, facts or citations to make text appear more human.
- Do not intentionally introduce spelling or factual errors.
- Do not optimize for defeating AI-detection systems; optimize for readable, authentic communication.
- Preserve domain terminology when simplification would change technical meaning.
