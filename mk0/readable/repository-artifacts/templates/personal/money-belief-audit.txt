# Money Belief Audit Template

**Artifact type:** template  
**Status:** derived-analysis  
**Source inspiration:** Alpacka AI Threads post `DPwn67yDrZK`  
**Official source:** https://www.threads.com/@alpacka.ai/post/DPwn67yDrZK

## Purpose

A structured reflection template for examining assumptions and recurring beliefs that may influence personal financial decisions.

This is not financial advice and does not attempt to diagnose psychological conditions.

## Template

```text
Help me examine the beliefs that may be shaping my relationship with money.

Context I can provide:
- financial goal: {{goal}}
- recurring situation or frustration: {{situation}}
- phrases about money I heard growing up, if relevant: {{background}}
- recent decisions I want to understand: {{decisions}}

Process:
1. Ask me up to five clarifying questions before drawing conclusions.
2. From my answers, identify up to three candidate beliefs or assumptions. Label them as hypotheses, not facts.
3. For each hypothesis, show:
   - evidence from what I actually said;
   - behavior it might encourage;
   - evidence that could contradict it;
   - a more useful alternative framing;
   - one low-risk experiment I can try this week.
4. Separate emotional reflection from financial recommendations.
5. Do not recommend investments, debt products or major financial actions unless I explicitly ask for that separately.

Output:
HYPOTHESIS
EVIDENCE
POSSIBLE EFFECT
COUNTER-EVIDENCE
ALTERNATIVE FRAME
SMALL EXPERIMENT
```
