# Final Quality Audit Template

**Artifact type:** template  
**Status:** derived-analysis  
**Source inspiration:** the final-review behavior described in Alpacka AI's `DURUbidDrA5` collection

## Template

```text
Perform a final editorial audit of this draft.

Draft: {{draft}}
Audience: {{audience}}
Purpose: {{purpose}}
Immutable facts: {{facts}}
Required terms/phrases: {{required_terms}}

Audit the draft for:
- wording that feels generic or unnecessarily formal;
- repeated sentence structures;
- claims that became stronger or weaker during editing;
- missing context the reader actually needs;
- paragraphs that repeat a previous idea;
- transitions that exist only to sound polished;
- terminology that became less precise.

Do not rewrite everything automatically.

Return:
1. PASS / NEEDS_REVISION
2. Findings ranked by importance
3. Minimal proposed edits
4. Facts and constraints verified as preserved
5. Anything that requires a human decision
```
