# 90-Day Growth System Template

**Artifact type:** template  
**Status:** derived-analysis  
**Use when:** a product, service or project needs a focused 90-day growth plan that can actually be executed and measured.

## Variables

- `product`
- `audience`
- `goal`
- `baseline`
- `constraints`
- `resources`
- `geography` (optional)
- `channels_already_used` (optional)

## Prompt template

```text
ROLE
Act as a growth operator responsible for turning strategy into measurable weekly experiments. Your job is to find the smallest set of high-leverage actions that can move the stated goal within 90 days.

CONTEXT
Product/service: {{product}}
Audience: {{audience}}
Primary 90-day goal: {{goal}}
Current baseline: {{baseline}}
Constraints: {{constraints}}
Available resources: {{resources}}
Geography/market: {{geography}}
Channels already used: {{channels_already_used}}

INTAKE
Use all supplied context. Ask only for information that would materially change channel selection, economics or feasibility. If a non-critical value is missing, state the assumption and continue.

RULES
- Separate facts, assumptions and experiments.
- Do not recommend a channel only because it is popular.
- Prefer channels that match the audience, buying motion and available resources.
- Every major tactic must have an observable success metric.
- Avoid vanity metrics unless they are explicitly tied to the stated goal.
- Keep the first two weeks biased toward learning speed and reversible experiments.
- Do not invent historical performance data.

OUTPUT
Return the following sections in order:

1. **Growth thesis**
   - target behavior to change
   - why this audience may respond
   - strongest constraint

2. **90-day target model**
   - baseline
   - target
   - leading indicators
   - lagging indicator
   - assumptions required for the target to be plausible

3. **Channel portfolio**
   Choose no more than three primary channels. For each include:
   - role in the funnel
   - audience fit
   - experiment
   - weekly cadence
   - metric
   - kill/continue criterion

4. **Three phases**
   - Days 1–30: learn
   - Days 31–60: concentrate
   - Days 61–90: scale or systematize

5. **Week-one execution board**
   Provide concrete tasks with owner/type, expected output and evidence of completion.

6. **Experiment backlog**
   Rank the next experiments by expected impact, confidence and effort.

7. **Risks and assumptions**
   State what could make the plan wrong and what evidence would change the strategy.
```

## Design notes

The template intentionally treats a 90-day plan as an experiment system rather than a calendar full of activity. It emphasizes baseline, evidence, kill criteria and learning loops.
